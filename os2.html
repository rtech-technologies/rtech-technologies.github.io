OS*2 Infrastructure

The sovereign kernel designed for R1 hardware. Built with transparency at its core, enabling direct silicon interaction without corporate gatekeeping.

System Architecture

OS*2 implements a "Zero-Layer" approach. By mapping system operations directly to hardware logic, we eliminate the latency and obfuscation found in traditional enterprise operating systems.

Direct Silicon Link

Low-level assembly mapping ensures every cycle is accounted for and verifiable.

User-Centric Policy

The kernel respects user property. No remote telemetry, no forced updates, no exceptions.
